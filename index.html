01

what I do

Plain, practical training on AI, built around the work your team already does.

Find the right course
what
Most people are already using AI tools at work, and most of them learned by guessing. I teach teams to use them well: to ask properly, to check what comes back, and to know what must never go in. You don't need a technical background, and there's no hype.
who for
HR teams, charities, hotels and hospitality, schools, law firms and public sector teams. Most of the people I train don't work in technology, and some are uneasy about AI. That's who the workshops are built for.
where
In person in London, on site anywhere else, or online.
languages
English or Italian.
afterwards
People know what these tools are good at, where they go wrong, and what should never be put into them.

courses

Six workshops, in order of where most teams start. Prices are per session, not per person.

All prices
  1. 01

    AI foundations for teams

    For: a whole team, including people who have never used these tools.

    Length
    Half day (3½ hours)
    Group
    Up to 15
    Format
    In person or online
    Language
    English or Italian
    Price
    £1,450

    Most people are already using AI tools, and most of them learned by guessing. This gives the whole team one shared starting point, using the tools and documents they already work with.

    What's covered
    • What these tools are doing when they answer, with no maths
    • Why an invented answer sounds exactly as confident as a correct one
    • Asking properly: people rewrite a real task from their own week and compare the results
    • What must never go in, and what to do instead when the material is sensitive

06

questions

What people usually ask before getting in touch.

Ask me something else

  • Does my team need any technical knowledge?

    No. The workshops start from what people already do at work, and every technical word gets explained.

  • Is it safe? What about our data?

    What not to put into AI tools is part of every course. During the workshop I follow your organisation's rules, and where real documents would be risky we use sample data instead.

  • Which AI tools do you cover?

    Whichever your team uses or is planning to use, such as ChatGPT, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Gemini or Claude. The habits I teach work across all of them.

  • Some of my team are nervous about AI. Is that a problem?

    Not at all, and it's common. The workshops are paced for mixed confidence. Nobody has to perform, and worries are treated as useful information, not resistance.

  • Can you work with our IT team and our policies?

    Yes. I'm happy to talk to IT or compliance beforehand so the workshop matches what's allowed and what's installed.

  • How much does it cost?

    A half-day session is £1,450 for up to 15 people. Prices are per session, not per person. See all prices. Every proposal states the full price before you commit to anything.

  • Do you deliver in Italian?

    Yes. Every course can be run in English or Italian, in person or online.

  • Can you adapt the workshop for accessibility needs?

    Yes. Tell me in advance and I'll adjust: materials sent beforehand, large print, extra breaks, captions online, or anything else that helps.

  • Do you work with training providers?

    Yes. I work as an associate trainer or subject expert, in English or Italian. In Italy I work with accredited training providers (enti di formazione), including on longer modular programmes.
